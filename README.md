# GoharMind

### Industrial AI & Engineering Decision-Support System by Artavan Technologies

GoharMind is an Industrial AI platform developed by **Artavan Technologies** to help engineers and industrial teams work with engineering documents, operational knowledge, process information, and industrial data through AI-assisted retrieval, analysis, and decision support.

It is designed for industrial environments where reliability, traceability, privacy, and engineering context are important.

> **Status:** Under active development. This public repository presents selected non-confidential product and research information. Production data, proprietary knowledge bases, models, and security-sensitive implementation details may remain private.

---

## What GoharMind Does

GoharMind is being developed to help industrial teams:

- Retrieve trusted engineering knowledge
- Search and understand technical documents
- Use Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Analyze operational and process information
- Investigate alarms and operational problems
- Support root-cause analysis
- Perform predictive and data-driven analysis
- Support engineering and operational decisions
- Run AI locally or within controlled industrial environments

- Engineering manuals and procedures

- Alarm and event history
- Equipment documentation
- Structured and unstructured files

## GoharMind + GoharTwin

GoharMind and **GoharTwin** are complementary products within the Artavan industrial intelligence ecosystem.

**GoharMind** provides AI-based knowledge intelligence, contextual retrieval, analysis, and engineering decision support.

**GoharTwin** provides Digital Twin capabilities for monitoring, modeling, analytics, simulation, prediction, and optimization.

Together, they are being developed to connect engineering knowledge, operational data, AI, and Digital Twin technologies.
